about me

Hi! I’m Maleeha.

I’m a Global Business and Digital Arts student at the University of Waterloo, designing for user experience, brands and social media.

Growing up, art was my way of putting my imagination onto paper. That curiosity turned into a love for design, and today I bring the same creativity to every project, looking for the balance between what looks good and what works.

I enjoy turning ideas into visuals that are easy to understand and fun to look at: interfaces, campaigns, graphics and short-form video.
