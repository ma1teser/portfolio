The goal

How might we bring more regulars through the door and build a following that keeps them coming back?

Who it's for

  • Audience 01

    Students & remote workers

    Looking for a cozy place to study or work, good Wi-Fi and a reason to stay for a second coffee.

  • Audience 02

    Neighbourhood regulars

    Value a friendly, familiar spot, local events and treats they can't get at a chain.

Campaign menu

  • Loyalty cardAt the counterBuy nine, get the tenth free, to turn visitors into regulars.
  • Local partnersCommunityPop-ups and shout-outs with nearby businesses.
